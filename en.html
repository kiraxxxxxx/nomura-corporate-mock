Leather goods OEM
from Tokyo, since 1923.

Nomura Seisakusho is a leather goods maker in Higashi-Ueno, Tokyo. We turn your ideas into leather goods — from planning and sampling to production and inspection.

Why Nomura

A century of leather craft, in Tokyo.

01

Sampling first

One of the largest sample rooms of any leather goods maker in Japan. We can make samples in several materials and designs, and refine them until you are satisfied.

02

Made in Tokyo

25 dedicated craftspeople, from their twenties to their seventies, in our own workshop in Higashi-Ueno, Tokyo — with first-class certification in a skills test for small leather goods. From planning to production, all under one roof.

03

Domestic or overseas production

Production in our Tokyo workshop or at our two partner factories overseas. Every product is inspected by our own staff.
